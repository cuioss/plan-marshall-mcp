== Overview

Defect archetypes that are not bound to one specification document: measurement and population
discipline, single sources for closed vocabularies, producer/consumer closure, guard and test fidelity,
claim verification, and change sequencing. Every PM-MCP component that reports a number, evaluates a
guard or ships a test is exposed to them. Item format and lifecycle: link:../ImplementationWatch.adoc[Implementation Watch].

== Measurement & Populations

[#PM-WATCH-GEN-1]
=== PM-WATCH-GEN-1: Every published figure names its population and provenance

Anchor:: link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14], link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-EXEC-6[PM-EXEC-6],
link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#post-run-quality-report[Phase Workflows § Post-Run Quality Report] (Instrument Contract)
Hazard:: plan-marshall reported confident ratios over wrong, pooled or empty populations: `reviewer_coverage: 0/3`
on a PR that had been split, a "measurable population 0" run graded anyway, a single-plan figure quoted as a
corpus band ("76–85% everywhere" where the per-phase values were 57.2–85.4%), "all findings addressed" claims
that were wrong for 9 findings across 3 phases, a sizing taken on the unchanged incumbent surface instead of
the changed one, a verdict of `nominal` over a channel only 5.8% complete, and timings measured under
concurrent agent load quoted as constants. Changing a ratio's population silently invalidated its thresholds.
Guard:: Every metric, ratio and report figure carries its numerator and denominator population, `n`, date range,
producing command or instrument version, and a withheld reason; `withheld`, `unmeasured` and `0` are distinct
values. A population of 0 renders `unmeasurable`, never a rate. Truncated lists carry a total beside the shown
items; all/none statements report `N of M`; verdicts carry their input completeness; rates are keyed by cause
and charter epoch and never pooled across them; performance figures state the host load. Changing a ratio's
population re-derives its thresholds on archived real data. Population fields are named by the axis they measure;
two `*_population` families over different axes are never compared as one population. A scan result (e.g.
contradiction incidence across findings stores) carries `records_scanned` and `stores_reached` of
`stores_expected`; a zero over fewer stores than expected renders `none reachable`, never `0`. Signal gates
(`adr-propose`, `lessons-capture`) record the window and population their count was computed over. Tests: every
rendered ratio is exercised with an empty population, `0/0` and a `1/1` roster.
Fixture:: `reviewer_coverage: 0/3` on PR #1419 after its split into #1423 and #1429 (7 CodeRabbit findings);
`structural_share` `null` at 1/3 becoming `100.0` at 1/1 after roster narrowing; "eight" flags passed per call
vs "nine" declared by the parser; 35 metrics files carrying `exploration_index_answerable_bytes` (earlier 13);
sizing 38/215 (incumbent surface) vs 31 source / 43 prose files (actual rename surface); crawl timings 1–5 s
over 9 unloaded measurements; an ~893K-token hole graded `nominal` at completeness 0.058. #1349: measurable
population 0, so no quality claim about the bots is supportable in either direction. Reviewer charter changed at

signal-gate count of 1 where the window held 2 distinct notations, computed over a window of 400 of 695 entries.

Anchor:: link:../specification/phase-workflows/05-finalize-review-metrics-descriptor.adoc#_build_time_startup_static_invariant_verification_finalizepipelinevalidator[Phase Workflows § Build-Time & Startup Static Invariant Verification],
