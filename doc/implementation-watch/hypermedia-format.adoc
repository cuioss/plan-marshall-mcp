== Overview

Watch items for the link:../specification/hypermedia-format.adoc[Hypermedia Format & TOON Encoding Specification]:
byte stability of resident content, summary budgets, summary/fact consistency, and multi-line values through the
encoder. The sections mirror the specification. Item format and lifecycle:
link:../ImplementationWatch.adoc[Implementation Watch].

== Normative TOON Representation Structure

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-1]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-1: Long-lived early bytes are byte-stable

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#_normative_toon_representation_structure[Hypermedia Format § Normative TOON Representation Structure],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-IMPL-3[PM-IMPL-3], link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-SKILL-2[PM-SKILL-2]
Hazard:: Bytes that enter a session early and stay resident dominate cost; rewriting them invalidates the host's
cached prefix.
Guard:: The tool list, skill catalogue and representation prelude render byte-identically for identical state; a test
asserts byte stability across calls. Shrink only early-entering long-lived bytes; never rewrite the cached prefix.
Source:: plan-marshall defect code-intelligence-substrate inbox other-approaches-001 § 3.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-2]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-2: The summary budget holds at worst-case expansion

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#canonical-top-level-fields[Hypermedia Format § Canonical Top-Level Fields] (`summary`),
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-5[PM-WF-5]
Hazard:: A composed status line exceeded 80 characters and was not ASCII.
Guard:: Property test over the maximum segment count times the maximum numeric width: the composed `summary` stays
within 160 ASCII characters; counts saturate (`99+`).
Fixture:: `0 comment(s) found — 1 empty, 1 refused, 1 refused-structural (unified triage pending)` (86 characters,
contains `—`).
Source:: plan-marshall defect review-apparatus PLAN-PR-026 D4.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-3]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-3: The summary never contradicts its facts

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#canonical-top-level-fields[Hypermedia Format § Canonical Top-Level Fields]
Hazard:: A step summary said "0 comment(s) found" while its detail listed 1 reviewed, 1 empty and 1
refused-structural bot.
Guard:: `summary` is rendered from the same state distribution as `facts`; a test asserts that a refused-structural
bot never yields a "0 found" summary.
Fixture:: #1488.
Source:: plan-marshall defect review-apparatus PLAN-PR-026 D3a.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-6]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-6: Integration identity and run details stay out of model-facing output

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#model-facing-minimality[Hypermedia Format § Model-Facing Minimality]
Hazard:: Operator-only details reach the model because they sit on the record being rendered: the agent identity,
the integration variant, CI run ids and URLs, or credential keys, kinds, and expiry, in a representation, a `pm://`
resource, a tool result, or the failure envelopes of `remediation-ci.gather` (`facts.failures`) and their evidence
resources.
Guard:: Contract test over every representation, resource, and tool result of fixture plans in both variants (GitHub
App, token, CLI), the failure envelopes of `remediation-ci.gather` and their evidence resources included: none
contains `agent_identity`, the variant, `operator_ambient`, a
credential key, `token_kind`, `expires_at`, a run id, or a provider run URL; the same values are present in
`audit.jsonl` and on the operator surfaces.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-7]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-7: The task, answer, and exit links sit where the process allows them

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#question-waiver-and-exit-links[Hypermedia Format § Task, Answer, Waiver-Request & Exit Links],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-3[PM-WF-3]
Hazard:: An exit link rendered at the wrong scope lets a model end a plan from a nested unit or step out where
nothing unwinds; an answer link offered to a worker lets a model answer its own question; a `consult` link without a
declaring clause reaches a role the workflow never named; `request_waiver` offered for a non-waivable gate files a
request no operator can answer.
Guard:: Representation tests over every catalogued link state and every task entity: no representation offers a link
that ends a plan or an epic; `step_out` (rel `abort`, form `submit`) appears only on resting link states of a
sub-workflow the entry session drives, never on a task entity or a view; `answer:<question_id>` appears only for the
entry session, never on a task entity; `consult:<role>` appears exactly once per `consult role` clause of the task's
state and never on a consulted task; `request_waiver` (rel `escalate`, form `submit`) only on the decision task of a
remediation unit with `waiver_wait_seconds > 0` and a waivable failing gate; the rel `escalate` admits no other form
and no other action.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-8]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-8: The wait-region matrix and the bot mapping are total

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/03-composite-wait-and-encoder.adoc#short-circuit-matrix[Hypermedia Format § Short-Circuit Matrix],
link:../specification/hypermedia-format/03-composite-wait-and-encoder.adoc#bot-status-mapping[Hypermedia Format § Per-Bot State Mapping],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-9[PM-WF-9]
Hazard:: A combination of `ci_status`, `analysis_status`, and per-bot states that no row matches leaves the
composite wait unsettled forever, or two rows match and the outcome depends on evaluation order; a per-bot state
without an aggregate mapping is read as `completed`.
Guard:: Property test over the full cross product of the closed enumerations (constituent fates included): exactly
one row matches first and names one declared outcome of `finalize.watch-ci-and-bots` (`watch_interrupted`,
`cancelled`, `ci_failed`, `analysis_failed`, `all_green`, `findings_open`, `bot_refused_awaitable`, `bot_refused_structural`,
`review_evidence_missing`, `bot_no_unreviewed_commit`) or keep-waiting; every per-bot state maps to one `bot_status`
value; an externally cancelled CI run settles `ci_failed`, an engine cancellation of the composite job (an operator
`abort`) `cancelled`.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-9]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-9: A workspace task carries no scope identifier and is addressed with `NO_PLAN`

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#representation-kinds[Hypermedia Format § Representation Kinds],
link:../specification/mcp-tools/01-core-workflow-tools.adoc#pm-wait[MCP Tools § `pm_wait` — Receive a Task],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-ARCH-4[PM-ARCH-4]
Hazard:: A task entity of the workspace scope that carries the `NO_PLAN` sentinel, or a made-up identifier, as its
`plan_id` invites the worker to treat the sentinel as a plan; a server that refuses `NO_PLAN` for a workspace task
leaves the worker no way to follow its links.
Guard:: Test over the `pm_wait` offer and `pm_state` of a task of the workspace scope (a `project-setup` task): the
representation carries neither `plan_id` nor `epic_id`; `pm_do` and `pm_state` with `plan_id: "NO_PLAN"` and the
task's `entity` are accepted from the worker holding the lease. Matched control: a task of a plan scope carries its
`plan_id`, and the same call with `NO_PLAN` for it is refused.
Source:: design review.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-10]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-10: Open: `recipe_params` outside the free-parameter grammar

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/02-link-grammar.adoc#_free_parameter_descriptor_grammar_free_column[Hypermedia Format § Free Parameter Descriptor Grammar]
Hazard:: Undecided: the grammar has no free-form object type, but `submit recipe_params` is an object validated against the
recipe's `parameters_schema`; project recipes bring uncompiled schemas, and `x-pm-options` are computed at runtime.
The link cannot be rendered or validated by the grammar, so it either bypasses the closed parameter validation or
cannot be offered. Options: (1) a grammar type for a per-recipe dynamic schema and its `free` rendering; (2) flatten
to typed scalar links rendered from the schema; (3) restrict `parameters_schema` to compiled recipes.
Recommendation: 1, it keeps project recipes and one validation path.
Guard:: The plan implementing this anchor decides the point (with the operator where it changes scope), records
the result in the specification and the requirement it touches, and deletes this item.
Source:: `hypermedia-format/02-link-grammar.adoc:102-107`, `domain-extensions/05-recipes-and-extension-points.adoc:19`, `:63`.

== Deterministic Native TOON Encoder Specification

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-4]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-4: Multi-line values survive the full hop

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/03-composite-wait-and-encoder.adoc#_encoding_rules[Hypermedia Format § Encoding Rules],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-IMPL-3[PM-IMPL-3], link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-TEST-2[PM-TEST-2]
Hazard:: 0.01% of a transcript was delivered, losing 121 of 123 operator turns and 7 of 7 gate-decision bodies; the
fix landed one hop downstream of the corrupting producer, and single-line fixtures let it ship (three independent
reports).
Guard:: Tests drive multi-line values (newlines, `#` headings, quotes) from the producer record through the TOON
encoder and `stdin` to the consumer and assert byte equality.
Fixture:: 725,532 bytes produced, 69 delivered.
Source:: plan-marshall defect post-run-quality PLAN-PRQ-12 D1.

[#PM-WATCH-HYP-11]
=== PM-WATCH-HYP-11: The encoder emits the official form, not a dialect

Anchor:: link:../specification/hypermedia-format/03-composite-wait-and-encoder.adoc#_encoding_rules[Hypermedia Format § Encoding Rules],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-IMPL-3[PM-IMPL-3]
Hazard:: An encoder built for a hand-picked subset drifts from the official specification where the subset is not
closed: a trailing newline, `key[0]:` for an empty array, a C0 control character not escaped as `\u00XX`, a quoted DEL, or a
map of uniform objects written as nested objects is valid-looking TOON that an official decoder reads differently or
refuses.
Guard:: The conformance test runs every encode fixture of the pinned release byte-exact and fails on any fixture
that is neither passed nor listed with an option outside the canonical options; the list is closed, so a fixture that
newly fails cannot be moved to it silently. Matched control: one fixture per form of Rules 5 to 10 and one per escape
of Rule 4 passes.
Fixture:: release v4.1.3: 185 encode fixtures; a subset encoder passed 114, refused 48 as outside the subset, and 23
set options.
Source:: roadmap/technical_macos.adoc M11.
