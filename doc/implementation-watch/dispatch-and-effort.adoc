== Dispatch Observability & Return Protocol

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-2]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-2: The legacy cost baseline ran dark

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#spend-accounting-budget-anchors[Dispatch & Effort § Spend Accounting & Budget Anchors],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-MIG-5[PM-MIG-5],

Hazard:: The plan-marshall cost instrument ran dark: every usage column of every dispatch row was unmeasured on
whole plans, so the legacy baseline the PM-MIG-5 comparison needs does not exist; an aggregate through a
zero-fabricating instrument had produced a retired headline figure ("a third of finalize spend").
Guard:: Measure the legacy baseline over a declared population before the Stage 1 cutover; alert when a dispatch

3,547,890 tokens dispatched, 0 of 11 rows measured, billing total 0 at population 0. Population available for a
baseline: n=5 plans at staging, n=1 six-phase record, 35–36 metrics files, 35 of them carrying
`exploration_index_answerable_bytes`.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-3]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-3: Dispatched tokens are not billing cost

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#_normative_schema_for_returneddispatch_id[Dispatch & Effort § Normative Schema for `returned:<dispatch_id>`],
link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#spend-accounting-budget-anchors[Dispatch & Effort § Spend Accounting & Budget Anchors],
link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-MIG-5[PM-MIG-5]
Hazard:: Reports presented dispatched tokens as plan cost, while billing tracks context residency multiplied by
turns and is dominated by cache reads; a 3-file change cost 71.4M billing tokens.
Guard:: Reports label which figure they show (dispatched vs billing) and never present dispatched tokens as plan
cost. The data below is the evidence base for any cost model (`cost = resident_context × turns`,
`cost(byte) ≈ 1.25 + 0.1 × turns_remaining`).
Fixture:: PLAN-TRUTH-098: 9,203,064 dispatched vs 185,422,539 billing (~20×); phase 6 4,996,549 / 96,178,416; 3
loop-backs; residency 209K → 771K. PLAN-TRUTH-075: +745/−2 over 3 files for 71.4M billing; 6-finalize 45.3M (63%),
342 tool uses, 80% idle; dispatch rows covered 19.3% (238,455 of 1,233,654). 842.6M `cache_read` / 18.9M
`cache_creation`; ~122-turn envelope; re-read factor 44.6×; 5.7× mean, 13.4× for an early byte. Composition
(plan-45): `cache_read` 73.15%, `cache_creation` 25.69%, output 1.13%, input 0.03% (n=47: 76.1 / 22.8 / 1.1);
recompute 66,212,048 vs 66,212,049; context ≈ 99% (99.08% for CIS-031). A 4-plan population spends 65.4% on
cache creation (population-blocked).
Source:: code-intelligence-substrate epic W-047, W-048, F7 and D-c, PLAN-CIS-054 D10, PLAN-CIS-036 D1.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-4]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-4: Exploration attribution is per phase and never pooled

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#_normative_schema_for_returneddispatch_id[Dispatch & Effort § Normative Schema for `returned:<dispatch_id>`]
Hazard:: A single-plan exploration share was quoted as a corpus band ("76–85% everywhere") and phases were pooled;
"unattributed" mixed two populations with different denominators.
Guard:: PM-MCP cannot observe host tool-result bytes; this applies only if attribution is computed, e.g. from a
transcript parser. Then the sub-sources sum exactly to the phase exploration bytes (absent excludes, a measured 0

pooled, re-entered phases are not double-counted, and index-answerable bytes are a lower bound.

PR #1126); per-phase exploration share 85.4 / 74.8 / 57.2 / 80.4 / 73.1%, worst phase 2-refine; unattributed bytes
18.9% (30.5% on #1086); `cache_read` unattributed 65.9%, at least 83% except finalize 40.5%; first six-phase
fixture `2026-08-09-self-review-resweeps-full-surface-every-round` with per-phase index/document/unattributed
triples.
Source:: code-intelligence-substrate PLAN-CIS-050 D5c and D5d, PLAN-CIS-054 D8 and D9, PLAN-CIS-036 D1 and D2,
PLAN-CIS-039 D1 and D4, epic F6.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-5]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-5: The cache-read attribution formula is exact

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#_normative_schema_for_returneddispatch_id[Dispatch & Effort § Normative Schema for `returned:<dispatch_id>`]
Hazard:: Residency attribution attributed the whole cache-read total, including sub-agent reads.
Guard:: If residency attribution is computed, it uses exactly
`attributable = max(0, cache_read_total − max(0, subagent_cache_read))`, split by residency weights, with a
zero-weight branch; the suite kills the mutation `attributable = total`.
Fixture:: the mutation `attributable = total` gave 2 failed / 6 passed.
Source:: code-intelligence-substrate PLAN-CIS-054 D1.1a and D1.1b.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-6]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-6: Two-ledger pairing is iterative

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#_post_return_integrity_invariants[Dispatch & Effort § Post-Return Integrity Invariants]
Hazard:: A recursive augmenting-path matcher failed at ~1,000 rows sharing one timestamp.
Guard:: Timestamp-sorted rows of two ledgers are paired by index-order first-fit (a convex bipartite graph, so

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-7]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-7: Spend is never classified by termination status

link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14]
Hazard:: plan-marshall counted 39% of a run's tokens as waste because the self-review firings ended with status

Guard:: Spend aggregations key on what a dispatch produced (submitted entity, findings filed), never on its
termination `status`; terminal-error spend is labelled a proxy, never "wasted"; a read-cost identity such as

Fixture:: lesson `2026-08-27-16-002`: `error_total_tokens` 2,323,435, 26 findings fixed.
Source:: post-run-quality PLAN-PRQ-08 D3; code-intelligence-substrate PLAN-CIS-050 D4a and D4d.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-16]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-16: A fail-closed epic gate needs a correct population and a recovery path

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort.adoc#_git_distributed_epic_ledger_per_file_store_layout_adr_024[Dispatch & Effort § Git-Distributed Epic Ledger] (Surface Disjointness Gate)
Hazard:: One unrelated indeterminate member blocked every candidate: sibling-epic staged specs and a plan-less live
entry were population members and blocked all emission. Parse failures produced empty populations that read as

plan-less entries are not population members, that indeterminacy is derived from each candidate's own surface

recovery link.
Fixture:: 95 of 581 sibling-epic-spec candidates across 25 epics plus one `NO_PLAN` live entry blocked emission;
94 indeterminate candidates, about half from a self-collision; 12 of 62 case-variant headings → 0 overlaps (69
after the fix); 154 table claims parsed to 0; partition 104 / 0 / 26 / 201 = 331.
