=== PM-WATCH-DISP-22: A staged spec is re-grounded before it is emitted

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort/04-epic-coordination.adoc#_why_epics_emit_plans_rather_than_dispatching_sub_agents[Dispatch & Effort § Why Epics Emit Plans Rather Than Dispatching Sub-Agents] (Re-Grounding Staleness at Emission),
link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort/04-epic-coordination.adoc#server-derived-landing-facts[§ Server-Derived Landing Facts, Spec Re-Grounding & Cross-Plan Locks] (item 2)
Hazard:: A staged spec decays against a moving tree; emitting it unchecked nearly re-shipped finished work.
Guard:: A test advances the default branch on a staged row's expected surface after its last re-grounding and asserts
that the `emit` representation shows `regrounded_at_sha`, `main_commits_since` and the warning fact; a row never
re-grounded carries the warning too; the `emit` link is never withheld.
Fixture:: two staged plans were one emit from re-shipping finished work; the oldest staged specs are the least
trustworthy.
Source:: code-intelligence-substrate epic F0.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-23]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-23: The landing commit is found under a squash merge queue

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort/04-epic-coordination.adoc#server-derived-landing-facts[Dispatch & Effort § Server-Derived Landing Facts, Spec Re-Grounding & Cross-Plan Locks] (Squash-Aware Landing Commit)
Hazard:: The landing-commit lookup relied on a two-parent merge commit, which never exists under a squash merge
queue.
Guard:: A test on a squash-merged landing locates the commit as the base-branch commit whose subject ends with
`(#<pr_number>)`; the merge-commit tier is never the only tier.
Fixture:: PR #1348 landed as `9aaf22d8f` with the single parent `a2be2691c`, so a two-parent merge-commit tier can
never fire under a squash merge queue.
Source:: code-intelligence-substrate PLAN-CIS-050 D3.i.

[#PM-WATCH-DISP-26]
=== PM-WATCH-DISP-26: The plan mailbox has its own leaf lock

Anchor:: link:../specification/dispatch-and-effort/04-epic-coordination.adoc#plan-mailbox[Dispatch & Effort § Plan Mailbox],
link:../specification/store-schemas/13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#lock-free-snapshot-reads[Store Schemas § Lock-Free Snapshot Reads, Transaction Locks & Leases] (leaf locks)
Hazard:: A mailbox appended under the plan lock inverts the lock order when an epic transaction writes it, and two
unsynchronized writers read the same last `message_id` and append duplicates.
Guard:: Concurrent `mailbox_send` and plan-side consumption acknowledgements keep every `message_id` unique; a torn
last line is ignored by readers; the lock manager accepts the order epic lock -> mailbox leaf lock and records no
`lock_order_violation`.
Source:: operator decision of 2026-09-29 (doc review); no plan-marshall defect record.
