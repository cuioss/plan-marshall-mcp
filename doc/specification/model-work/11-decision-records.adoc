[#decision-records]
== Decision Records
_See Requirement link:../../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-19[PM-WF-19: Uniform Decision Records & Corpus Export]_
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-46[PM-WATCH-DISP-46]_

Every decision a scope takes at a decision point is recorded once, in one format, whoever takes it: a server rule that compares a measured value with a threshold, a task of a role that chooses from a closed set, a quorum of roles, or the user who answers a question. The *decision record* names the decider, the evidence the decision rests on, the options, the confidence or the measured values with the thresholds applied and their sources, the choice, and the rationale. A later event that departs from a decision appends a correction to it and never rewrites it (<<decision-corrections>>). The records serve two ends: every threshold is checked against the values it was applied to, and the operator gets real judgments with the decision finally taken, to add to the model verification corpus (<<corpus-export>>). The store and the schema are specified in link:../store-schemas/18-decision-records.adoc[Store Schemas Specification, Decision Records].

* *Not the audit log*: Decision records are scope records like question records. They are not part of the security audit trail (link:../cli-and-security/02-credentials-and-operator.adoc#_security_audit_trail_three_tier_identity_verification[CLI, Coordination & Security Specification, Security Audit Trail]), which keeps recording every transition and operator action; a decision record cites an operator action (a waiver) by its id only.
* *Never transmitted*: The records, their facts snapshots, and every export stay on the workstation (`PM-ARCH-2`); nothing is sent anywhere.
* *Written in the deciding transaction*: The record is appended in the transaction that takes the decision, so a decision that took effect always has its record, and a rolled-back transaction leaves none.

[#decision-record-content]
=== Content

[cols="2,5"]
|===
|Field |Content

|`decision_id`
|The next value of the scope-wide decision counter (`D-0001`, link:../identifiers/01-general-rules-and-registry.adoc#id-decision-id[Identifier Registry, `decision_id`]), never reused.

|`decision_point`, `state_id`, `subject`
|The decision point (<<decision-points>>), the state that took the decision, and what was decided: a question (`question_id`), a finding group, a trigger with its evidence key, a remediation call with its policy, a plan task, or the plan.

|`decision_kind`
|One kind of the closed set of link:10-decision-panel.adoc#decision-kinds[Decision Kinds]: a question decision takes its question's kind, every other decision the fixed kind of its decision point.

|`decider`
|`server_rule` (the rule: the primitive that decided), `role` (role, harness, model, effort, and `queue_task_id` of the task), `quorum` (every vote, <<question-decisions>>), or `user` (the answer channel: `web`, `session`, or `elicitation`). The operator is never a decider: an override is the operator's act on the operator channel, recorded as a correction that cites the waiver.

|`evidence`
|`facts_digest`, the SHA-256 of the facts the decider was given (for a judgment, their snapshot is kept, <<facts-snapshot>>), and the `pm://` URIs of the evidence the decision rests on, as the facts named them (link:06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-evidence[Evidence in Facts]).

|`options`
|The closed set the decision chose from: the offered actions or remedies, the option keys, or the values a rule chooses between. A question decision records `options: null` and names its question in `subject`: the question record owns the question, its options, and the asker's recommendation with its confidence and rationale.

|`confidence`
|A role decider: the `confidence` its closing link carried (<<decision-confidence>>). A quorum: the lowest confidence of its votes. `null` for a user and a server rule.

|`measurements[]`
|Every measured value the decision compared: its name, the value, the comparator, the threshold, and the threshold's source: `compiled` (a compiled default), `unit` (a declaration of the project-owned unit that takes the decision, with the unit's id and digest, link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#unit-declarations[Workflow DSL Specification, Unit Declarations]), `team_config` (a team configuration file), `local_config` (a value of the operator's `local-config.json` the rule read), `call_argument` (a bound argument of the plan instance), or `waiver` (a ceiling raised by a waiver, with its `waiver_id`). A decision without a threshold records the values it read, with the threshold `null`. Because every threshold carries its source, decisions of projects whose units declare other values stay comparable.

|`choice`, `reason`
|The option taken, and the closed reason code where the decision point has one (the adjudication reasons, `role_unqualified` of a conservative fallback). A decision that hands the choice on (an adjudication that delivers its set, a rule that leaves the choice to a task) records `choice: null` and its reason.

|`rationale`
|The decider's own words: a role's or a vote's rationale, the user's optional answer `text`, or the rule's composed statement of the values it compared.

|`head_sha`, `follows`, `at`
|The plan branch's `HEAD` when the decision was taken (`null` before the worktree exists and outside a plan), the decision this one completes (the user's answer to a set the adjudication delivered), and the time.
|===

[#decision-confidence]
=== Decision Confidence of a Task

Every closing link of a task whose state is a decision point of a role (`outline.classify-change-type`, `<phase>.qgate`, `replan.review`, `remediation-<class>.choose`) carries the parameter `confidence:int[0..100]` besides its own parameters (link:../hypermedia-format/02-link-grammar.adoc#decision-confidence-parameter[Hypermedia Format Specification, Decision Confidence Parameter]): the worker's confidence that its choice is right. A submit without it is refused `invalid_link_args`. The confidence is recorded and shown; it never gates the decision, so every role decision has a calibrated value before any threshold is set on it. A triage submit carries one `confidence` per finding group in its `triage-groups` items (link:07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#batched-triage[Batched Triage]), and each group's decision record takes its own group's confidence.

[#question-decisions]
=== Question Decisions & Quorum

A question's decision records sit beside its question record; the question record keeps the question, its delivery, its deadline, and the answer as received, and points to them (link:../store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Store Schemas Specification, Question Record]):

* *Adjudication*: In mode `panel` the adjudication writes one `question.adjudicate` record per question of the set in its ending transaction (link:10-decision-panel.adoc#adjudication-record[Ask or Quorum, Record]): the decider `quorum` with one vote per listed role (the role with its harness, model, `branch_key`, confidence, and rationale, and for a vote task its `queue_task_id` and task outcome) when votes were taken, else `server_rule` with the check's reason; every vote's confidence against the declared score and the agreeing votes against the listed roles as measurements; the agreed option with the reason `agreement`, or `null` with the escalation reason. Each question's `adjudicated` line names its record.
* *Answer*: A question that closes `answered` other than through a quorum gets one `question.answer` record in the transaction of its `answered` line: the decider `user` with its channel, or `server_rule` with the rule `question.fallback` (expiry) or `question.mode-none` for an `assumption` closure; it `follows` the question's adjudication record where one exists. The `answered` line names the record of the decision finally taken: the `question.answer` record, or the adjudication record of a `decided` set.
* *Choice repeated once*: The record repeats the answered `branch_key` as its `choice`, so every decision record holds its choice; the question record stays the owner of the question and the options.

[#facts-snapshot]
=== Facts Snapshot

The facts of every judgment decision (a role's decision and every question decision) are kept: when the server composes the facts of a decision task's entity, or of a question state for a server-raised question, it writes their canonical TOON text once, content-addressed by its SHA-256, to the scope's facts snapshot directory (link:../store-schemas/18-decision-records.adoc#facts-snapshot-store[Store Schemas Specification, Facts Snapshot]); the decision record names it by `facts_digest`. A question decision takes the facts of the asking task's entity at the time of the `ask` or the submit. The snapshot holds what the decider saw and nothing else: the facts are composed redacted, untrusted text stays inside its spotlighting delimiters, and evidence too large for facts stays a `pm://` URI. A server-rule decision keeps no snapshot: its measurements are its evidence.

[#decision-corrections]
=== Corrections

A decision is never rewritten. When a later event departs from it, the server appends one correction naming the decision, the cause, the new choice, and what caused it; every reader folds the corrections of a record, in order, into its `corrections[]`:

[cols="2,5"]
|===
|Cause |Appended when

|`reverted`
|A later decision of the same decision point on the same subject takes another choice (a finding group triaged again with another disposition, a light gate that runs again on a revised outline and goes `deep`), or the lane ratchet sends a plan `deep` against its `lane.budget` decision `light`; the reference is the later decision or the ratchet's trigger.

|`replanned`
|A re-planning names the decision in its submit: every action of `replan.review` takes an optional `corrects:list<text>?` of at most ten `decision_id` values of the plan the revision departs from (each validated, an unknown id refused `invalid_link_args`); `replan.apply` appends the corrections with the re-planning record as reference and the new choice `null`, because a revision holds no choice among the decision's options.

|`answered_differently`
|A question names the decision it revisits (`revisits` of the question envelope, link:06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-envelope-schema[Question Envelope]; the revisited decision's `choice` must be one of the question's option keys, else `invalid_link_args`), and the decision finally taken on the question chooses another option; the reference is that question decision.

|`overridden`
|An operator waiver is consumed for a gate a decision closed (a `blocking` Q-Gate verdict of the phase under `qgate_findings:<phase>`, the groups whose findings a `findings:<class>` waiver accepts); the reference is the `waiver_id`, the new choice `null`, and the decider of the correction `operator`. An override is the operator's policy, never a judgment of the decision: it changes no export target (<<corpus-export>>).
|===

[#threshold-calibration]
=== Thresholds Are Calibrated Defaults

Every threshold of a decision point is a compiled default, a declaration of the project-owned unit that takes the decision (within compiled bounds, link:../workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#unit-declarations[Workflow DSL Specification, Unit Declarations]), or the configuration key it is; no threshold is read from decision records at run time, and no decision point adjusts its own threshold. The declared thresholds (the re-planning thresholds and budget, link:../phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#replan-constants[Declared Thresholds]; the listed roles and score of the adjudication's quorum declarations, link:10-decision-panel.adoc#decision-declarations[Ask or Quorum, Decision Declarations]) and the compiled ones (the light gate's cap and band, the change-type heuristic's `0.70`, the recipe auto-route `0.6`, and the refine `confidence_threshold`) are defaults whose calibration rests on the decision records: every evaluation records the measured value against the threshold in force with its source, and later corrections show where the rule decided against what followed. The calibration export (<<corpus-export>>) gives the operator these rows; a team changes a declared threshold by a reviewed edit of its unit, and a changed shipped value reaches a project as an offered default change of the unit (link:../workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#project-owned-units[Workflow DSL Specification, Project-Owned Units], item 6).

[#corpus-export]
== Corpus Export (`pm-operator decisions export`)
_See Requirements link:../../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-19[PM-WF-19: Uniform Decision Records & Corpus Export], link:../../Requirements.adoc#PM-WORK-6[PM-WORK-6: Role Qualification]_
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-46[PM-WATCH-DISP-46]_

The operator verb `pm-operator decisions export` (link:../cli-and-security/02-credentials-and-operator.adoc#operator-decisions-export[CLI, Coordination & Security Specification, Decision Export]) writes selected judgment decisions as `draft` items of the model verification corpus to a directory the operator names. It is never run automatically, writes nothing into the corpus itself, and transmits nothing: the operator reviews each item and copies the ones worth keeping into the model verification corpus `test/model/verification/` as its README describes (link:../evaluation.adoc#corpus[Evaluation Reference, Verification Corpus]).

* *Selection*: The project's plan, epic, and workspace decision stores, archived plans included, filtered by `--kind` (decision kinds), `--point` (decision points), and `--since` (the decision time). A decision is exported when it is a judgment of an exportable point (<<export-mapping>>), its facts snapshot is present, and its kind is not `external_fact` (a fact only the user holds would grade a model on what it cannot see).
* *Target*: The decision finally taken: the choice of the last correction of cause `reverted` or `answered_differently` where one exists, otherwise the decision's own choice. A decision whose last such correction is `replanned`, or chooses outside the decision's options, is not exported, because the choice that would have been right is not recorded. An `overridden` correction leaves the target unchanged and is named in the item's review notes. A question closed `assumption` without such a correction is not exported, because its target would be the asker's own recommendation.
* *Input*: The question (the question text, or the decision point's fixed question of <<export-mapping>>), the options as `input.choices` (option keys, with their labels and descriptions in the facts), and as `input.facts` the facts snapshot followed by the content of every evidence URI the record names that still resolves in the project's stores, each quoted as data; a URI that does not resolve is named as `evidence unavailable` and the item is tagged `evidence-incomplete`. The input never carries the asker's recommendation, its confidence or rationale, a vote, or the choice: like a vote task's input, it is blind (link:10-decision-panel.adoc#blind-input[Blind Input]).
* *Metadata*: `provenance.kind` `extracted`, `created` the export date, `origin` with the project's repository (when it is a GitHub repository), the plan's `head_sha` as `commit`, and as `record` the plan id and the `decision_id`; `history` states in plain words who decided, at which confidence or measured value against which threshold, and every correction with its cause; `tags` carry `decision-point:<point>`, `decision-kind:<kind>`, `decider:<kind>`, and `confidence:<n>`; `review.status` `draft`, with notes that say whether the target is a user's answer, a correction, or an unconfirmed decision of a role or a quorum; `canary` the corpus canary; `grading.method` `exact_choice`.
* *Files*: One item per decision, `<out>/<topic>/x-<plan_id>-<decision_id>.json` (lower case), validated against the corpus schema before it is written; an existing file is never overwritten. The verb prints the items written and the decisions skipped with their reason (`no_facts`, `kind_excluded`, `assumption`, `corrected_without_choice`, `not_exportable`).
* *Calibration rows*: With `--calibration` the verb also writes `<out>/calibration/<decision_point>.jsonl`: one row per server-rule decision with its measurements, choice, reason, corrections, and the plan's outcome. Server-rule decisions are calibration data and never corpus items.

[#decision-visibility]
=== Reading Decisions

The decisions of a plan are read as `pm://plan/{plan_id}/decisions`, those of an epic as `pm://epic/{epic_id}/decisions`, and those of the workspace scope as `pm://workspace/decisions` (link:../mcp-tools/03-resource-uris.adoc[MCP Tool & Resource Surface Specification, Resource URIs]), each record with its corrections folded; the web app shows them in the scope's view (link:../cli-and-security/03-web-server.adoc#web-app[CLI, Coordination & Security Specification, Web App]). The pull-request body and the landing record list no decision records of their own: where they list answered questions and amendments, each entry names its `decision_id`.
