=== Finalize Step Ordering Bands

The Phase 6 (`finalize`) pipeline is a banded integer `order` space partitioned around the single `push` barrier (`order = 11`) and the `branch-cleanup` merge gate (`order = 70`).

==== Band Allocation Summary

[cols="2,1,2,4"]
|===
|Band Name |`order` Range |Owner |Semantic Role & Insertion Guarantee

|*Settle — Pre-Push*
|`1..10`
|Shared bundle + project-local (densely packed at `3..10`)
|Prepares, reviews, mutates source, refreshes architecture, and runs the final local quality gate before pushing. `pre-push-quality-gate` (`10`) is strictly the last step before `push` (`11`). Adding a new step inside `3..10` requires a deliberate re-spacing of the sub-cluster.

|*Push Barrier*
|`11`
|Shared bundle (fixed)
|Single branch push (`default:push`), implemented via `call git.commit_and_push (message_mode=none_assert_clean, push_mode=if_push_remote)`. Verifies the `worktree_sha` freshness gate, clean working tree, and remote branch parity before pushing.

|*Settle — Post-Push & Concurrent Wait Region*
|`12..69`
|Shared majors (`20`, `22`, `30`, `40`, `62`) + interior gaps (`12..19`, `21`, `23..29`, `31..39`, `41..61`, `63..69`)
|Opens the PR (`20`), enters the composite concurrent wait region (`22..40`: CI verification, automatic review bots, Sonar analysis) with the unified triage hook, and proposes ADRs (`62`). Any source fix via unified triage or loop-back re-enters the Settle band.

|*Merge Gate*
|`70`
|Shared bundle (fixed)
|`default:branch-cleanup` — runs the merge path (xref:06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#merge-path[Merge Path (`branch-cleanup`)]): acquires the cross-plan merge mutex, rebases onto base only when required, evaluates the pre-merge review barrier, merges via safe-merge or merge-queue bound to the expected head SHA, destroys the worktree (`destroys: [worktree]`), and clears `worktree_path` in the same transaction that records the removal.

|*Post-Merge Operational*
|`71..899`
|Project-local / third-party
|Post-merge operational actions that do not mutate tracked source and are not backward-looking reports. (Legacy `deploy-target` at `81` and `sync-plugin-cache` at `85` are obsolete for `PM-MCP`'s own distribution.)

|*Post-Run Review*
|`900..999`
|Shared bundle (`991..999`) + reserved gap (`900..989`)
|Backward-looking analysis and reporting steps (`post_run_review: true`, `mutates_source: false`) that depend on post-merge evidence: `lessons-capture` (`991`), `preference-emitter` (`992`), optional `plan-retrospective` (`995`), `record-metrics` (`998`), `print-phase-breakdown` (`999`).

|*Terminal Emission*
|`1000..1099`
|Shared bundle (reserved)
|`default:emit-landing` (`1000`, `post_run_review: true`) — emits the machine-readable landing envelope to the orchestrator epic inbox after all metrics and post-run reports complete.

|*Terminus*
|`1100`
|Shared bundle (fixed)
|`default:archive-plan` (`1100`) — archives/moves the completed plan directory (`destroys: [plan-directory]`). Strictly the highest `order` in the pipeline.
|===

The catalogue mirrors the finalize step roster of `plan-marshall` (step identifiers, `order`, descriptor facts, `lane` data, and defaults), verified against `plan-marshall` commit `1d4a1da37` (`extension-api/standards/ext-point-finalize-step.md` and each step's frontmatter). Differences are marked as *deliberate changes*. Before implementing the finalize pipeline, re-verify the roster against the latest `plan-marshall` version. Rows marked "project" are project-local steps of `plan-marshall`'s own repository, listed only to show how project steps occupy the bands; they are not shipped.

The catalogue is the roster of the binary's *default* `finalize.pmwf` unit. A project runs its own copy (`.marshall/config/finalize.pmwf`, link:../workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#project-owned-units[Workflow DSL Specification, Project-Owned Workflow Units & Compiled Invariants]): it may omit an optional step (lane class `prunable` or `adversarial`, or a step whose default is off) and add project steps (descriptor in `workflow.json` `plan.phase-6-finalize.project_steps`, script bound in the enrolment), but every step of lane class `core` or `derived-state` (the mandatory steps, xref:02-planning-lanes-phases-3-4.adoc#_shared_execution_manifest_posture_preview_resolver[Shared Execution Manifest & Posture Preview Resolver]) must be present at its catalogue `order`, and the verified unit decides the roster; `workflow.json` only sets the lane and parameters of an optional step.

Every `head_dependent` step declares its `input_scope` (xref:06-finalize-re-entry-and-merge.adoc#verdict-input-scope[Verdict Input Scope]): `remote_state` for `ci-verify`, `automatic-review`, and `sonar-roundtrip`; `open_ended` for `pre-push-quality-gate`; `plan_footprint` for `simplify`, `security-audit`, and `pre-submission-self-review`. Project-local steps declare their own (`plan-marshall`'s `plugin-doctor` has no sound subset, `open_ended`; its `era-stamp-fill` names its files, `declared`). *Deliberate change vs. `plan-marshall`*: `plan-marshall` has no `input_scope` field; its steps either declare `verdict_inputs` or leave it absent.

A delta round never closes the step: a clean delta round is followed by exactly one full-surface round over the whole plan diff, and only a full-surface clean round records `may_close = true`; a delta round that surfaces findings loops back without a confirmation round. *Review chain terminus*: after the last remediation commit that follows the final review-bot round, the engine runs exactly one self-review pass over the residual delta since the last bot-reviewed head; its findings are filed and shown to the operator at the pre-merge gate, never auto-fixed, and no further chaining occurs.

|`core`, on; `requires: [ci-complete]`
|Monitors CI workflow runs against pushed `HEAD` (`input_scope: remote_state`), looked up by head SHA and re-polled at the provider re-poll cadence (link:../timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#ci-repoll-cadence[Timeouts & Budgets Specification]); check runs owned by registered review bots are excluded. Applies a deterministic two-level CI failure taxonomy to every failed check: `failure_class` from the provider's conclusion (`failure`, `cancelled`, `timed_out`, `action_required`, `stale`, `startup_failure`, `not_triggered`, `no_checks`) and `failure_kind` from the check's log, parsed by the build output parsers of `PM-IMPL-6` (`compile`, `test`, `lint`, `policy`, or `unknown` when no parser recognises the log or the log is unreadable). `unknown` stays its own value and is handled fail-closed: its finding is blocking like any other. For producers declaring a `verification_profile`, runs an adversarial refutation sub-state (`ext-point-verify`) first. Model submits `triage:F-n` (`disposition: enum[FIX\|SUPPRESS\|ACCEPT]`, `reply_text: text[1..4000]`, optional `requirement_refs: list<text[1..120]>`). The `finalize.triage` representation carries every open finding's essentials in `facts.findings` (`finding_id`, `source`, `bot_kind`, `severity`, `blocking`, `summary`, `file_path`, `line`, and the `provider_ref.comment_kind`), so triaging N findings costs exactly N `pm_do` calls and no reads; the untrusted `body` stays behind the `pm://plan/{plan_id}/findings` resource, and a table beyond the render bound names the omitted count and that resource. `requirement_refs` names the requirement ids (`PM-<AREA>-<N>`) or specification anchors the finding bears on; the server validates each against the requirement and specification registry of the binary and refuses an unknown reference with `invalid_link_args`. `FIX` transitions via `loop_back` to Phase 5 (if cross-task) or settles in-branch via `finalize.settle-commit` and re-runs from the Settle band; server transmits replies/dismissals via `stdin` or REST to GitHub/GitLab/Sonar. `finalize.triage` exits automatically once the last open finding is triaged: with at least one `FIX` to the in-branch fix path (`finalize.settle-commit`), otherwise to `finalize.next-step`, completing the wait-region steps as `done` (link:../state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#state-finalize.triage[State Catalogue]).

The triage `submit` is refused when the chosen disposition contradicts the finding's own detail classification (checked at the write seam, never coerced from prose). `FIX` carries `population: text[1..2000]` naming the sites the fix covers (the whole co-reference population of the defect, never only the named site), recorded on the finding. A disposition that lowers a finding's severity must cite an executed check (build or test job id); raising it needs only a rationale. A disposition whose remedy is documentary (`reply_text` promising a change) is recorded as a commitment `{finding_id, promised_change, anchor_paths}` and re-checked against the merge-candidate diff before the barrier; an unmet commitment fails Predicate 1, and reconciliation over zero commitments records `not_reached` with `population: 0`. When the `ext-point-verify` refutation sub-state is unavailable, the triage records the substitute evidence the resolution rested on (`evidence.class: substitute`). The disposition grammar stays `FIX\|SUPPRESS\|ACCEPT`; the server derives the finding's `resolution` (`fixed`, `rejected`, `suppressed`, `accepted`, `taken_into_account`) from the recorded evidence, the refutation verdict, and the finding's `scope_class` against the plan footprint (link:../store-schemas.adoc[Store Schemas Specification], Server-Written Resolution). A re-disposition of a finding whose reply was already transmitted requires a new `reply_text`; a bare re-disposition is refused. The triage facts carry round-scoped counters (replies sent, threads resolved) and the explicit `unanswered[]` list (`finding_id`, `transmit_outcome`, `transmit_reason`; link:../store-schemas.adoc[Store Schemas Specification], Transmit Reasons), empty when every finding was answered.
