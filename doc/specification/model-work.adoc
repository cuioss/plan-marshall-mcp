== Overview

This document specifies how *plan-marshall-mcp* (`PM-MCP`) gets model work done inside the plans and epics it drives. Every state that needs a model creates a task of a role; a worker of that role does it through the task protocol. A worker is a headless harness process that the runtime launches as a job of kind `llm`, supervises, recycles, and replaces; the interactive session may take tasks of reading roles as an optional worker, and a plan never waits on it.

The parts specify the role artifacts, the role sets and the one model caller, role qualification and the conservative fallback; the task protocol (`pm_wait`, offer, acknowledgement, submit, leases per generation, fencing, the `worker-task` sub-workflow); supervision and recycling; the launch of a worker; consultation between roles through the server; task envelopes, question records, and evidence in facts; the recommendation score of questions and their adjudication by declarations per decision kind (`ask` or a quorum of roles); the uniform decision records of every decision point and their export as corpus items; the screening of untrusted text; epic coordination; and task completion, usage, and spend. The measurements behind the values are recorded in the link:evaluation.adoc[Evaluation Reference]; the principles in the link:../concepts/harness-as-worker/README.adoc[Harness as Worker] concept.
