[#decision-record]
== Decision Record (`decisions.jsonl`)
_See Requirement link:../../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-19[PM-WF-19: Uniform Decision Records & Corpus Export]_
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/model-work.adoc#PM-WATCH-DISP-46[PM-WATCH-DISP-46]_

Every decision a scope takes at a decision point is one `decided` line of the scope's decision store; a later departure from it is one `corrected` line that names it. The semantics of every field, the decision points, the causes of a correction, and the export are owned by link:../model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#decision-records[Model Work Specification, Decision Records]; this section fixes the store. The store is append-only, one file per scope: `plans/<plan_id>/decisions.jsonl`, `epics/<epic_id>/decisions.jsonl`, and `workspace/decisions.jsonl` (link:../filesystem-layout.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store]). A line is appended in the transaction that takes the decision or the correction, under the scope lock and covered by the scope's digest sidecar like every scope store; no line is ever rewritten or removed.

* *Identifier*: `decision_id` is the next value of a scope-wide counter, zero-padded to four digits, never reused (link:../identifiers/01-general-rules-and-registry.adoc#id-decision-id[Identifier Registry, `decision_id`]). A correction carries the `decision_id` of the decision it corrects and has no identifier of its own.
* *Question link*: A question record's `adjudicated` and `answered` lines name their decision records by `decision_id` (xref:14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Question Record]).
* *Untrusted text*: `rationale` of a role, a panel system, or a user is model- or user-authored text; it is shown as text, never as markup, and it reaches a model only inside the facts of a later task, quoted as data.
* *Retention*: The decision store and the facts snapshots are part of the scope's store: a plan's move to the archive with `archive-plan`, its purge by the retention of archived plans, and `delete-plan` take them along (link:../filesystem-layout.adoc#archives[Filesystem Layout Specification, Plan & Epic Archives]). The epic and workspace stores are kept like their question records.

[#facts-snapshot-store]
=== Facts Snapshot (`decision-facts/<facts_digest>.toon`)

The facts of a judgment decision (link:../model-work/11-decision-records.adoc#facts-snapshot[Model Work Specification, Facts Snapshot]) are written once per distinct content to `decision-facts/<facts_digest>.toon` of the scope's store, where `facts_digest` is the SHA-256 of the canonical TOON text the file holds; the file is immutable, an existing file is never written again, and the digest sidecar covers it. A snapshot whose task closed without a decision (a task `lost`) is kept with the others; snapshots go only with their scope's store.
