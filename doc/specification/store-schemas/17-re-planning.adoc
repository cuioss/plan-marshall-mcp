[#replan-record]
== Re-Planning Record (`mcp/status.json` → `replan_pending`, `replans[]`, `follow_ups[]`)
_See Requirement link:../../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-17[PM-WF-17: Dynamic Re-Planning of a Running Plan]_

The plan's `mcp/status.json` (xref:02-workflow-state-store.adoc#_workflow_state_store_mcpstatus_json[Workflow State Store]) carries the state of its re-plannings (link:../phase-workflows/08-dynamic-re-planning.adoc#dynamic-re-planning[Phase Workflows Specification, Dynamic Re-Planning]): `replan_pending`, the trigger a checkpoint raised and the unit has not yet judged (`null` otherwise); `replans[]`, append-only, one record per judged trigger, whose evidence keys decide whether a checkpoint raises a trigger again and whose task count is charged against `replan_budget`; and `follow_ups[]`, append-only, the follow-up plans the re-plannings recorded. All three are written only by the server, in the transactions of the trigger points and of the `replan` unit.

* *Budget*: `replan_budget` is charged per record with `decided_by: task` or with a `queue_task_id` (a task that closed `lost` counts); a fallback taken without a task is not charged.
* *Rendering*: The pull-request body and the landing record list every record with its action and rationale, the moved deliverables, and the follow-ups with their scope and preserved ref; the epic's queue row of a follow-up carries its origin (xref:04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-queue-row[Queue Row], `follow_up`).
