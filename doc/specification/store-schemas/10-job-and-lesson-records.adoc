[#job-record]
== Job Record (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/jobs/<job_id>.json`)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/job-runtime.adoc#PM-WATCH-JOB-22[PM-WATCH-JOB-22], link:../../implementation-watch/job-runtime.adoc#PM-WATCH-JOB-23[PM-WATCH-JOB-23]_

The write-ahead job journal: one record per job, written at three points (four for a job that waits for a slot), each an atomic replace under the lock of the scope that owns the job (the plan or epic lock, or the workspace lock for a job without a plan or epic; for a worker, which belongs to the project's task queue, the queue lock `locks/queue.lock`):

. *Queued*: a job that must wait for a build slot or a worker that must wait for a model slot (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#machine-lease-stores[Machine Lease Stores]) is recorded first with `status: queued`, `spawned_at: null`; its budget has not started. It becomes `running` in the transaction that acquires its slot.
. *Write-ahead*: before `ProcessBuilder.start()`, with `status: running`, `pid: null`, and `process_started_at: null` (for an in-process `git` or `ci` operation of the native variant, before the operation starts; link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#in-process-operations[Job Runtime Specification, In-Process Operations]).
. *Post-spawn*: mandatory for a `process` job: the launcher reports its PID before `execve`, and the record is replaced with `pid`, `process_started_at`, and `spawned_at` before the job counts as running (before it enters `active_jobs` or a result reports it `running`).
. *Fate*: replaced at fate (link:../domain-extensions/06-configuration.adoc#_tier_3_adaptive_build_timeouts_crash_replay_state[Domain Extensions Specification, Tier 3], link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc#outcome-derivation[Job Runtime Specification, Outcome Derivation]).

*Write order at job end*: the job record's fate (for a worker in the same transaction as its fence: generation fenced, lease of a held task released, xref:16-task-queue.adoc#queue-task-record[Queue Task Record]), then the change-ledger row (xref:04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#_change_ledger_record_change_ledger_jsonl[Change Ledger Record (`change-ledger.jsonl`)]), then the timing observation (xref:09-derived-facts-and-build-timings.adoc#build-timings-store[Build Timings Store (`<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/build-timings.json`)]), then the slot release (a build slot, or the model slot of a worker, xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#machine-lease-stores[Machine Lease Stores]). A worker's token is revoked in the transaction that records its fate. A crash in between loses at most a freshness mark (the build runs again) or a timing sample; no marker records the progress and nothing is re-derived. The usage of each attempt of a task is a metrics row of the task's scope, written when the attempt ends (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store (`metrics.json`)]); the row of an attempt that ends with the worker is written in the worker's write order at job end (link:../job-runtime/01-pipeline-and-subprocesses.adoc[Job Runtime Specification, Outcome Derivation]).

*Recovery*: The project's recovery after a runtime restart marks every record left `queued` as `status: ended`, `fate: killed` without signalling (it never started), and reads every record left `running` (link:../runtime-model/02-startup-recovery-drain.adoc#_startup_sequence[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Startup]): a record whose `spawned_at` lies before the system boot time is marked `ended`/`killed` and nothing is signalled; a record with `pid: null` is marked `ended`/`killed` with the audit note `orphan unidentifiable` and nothing is signalled; every other record gets the same-boot process-group probe and termination (link:../job-runtime.adoc[Job Runtime Specification]). Retention removes records with their logs after `job_logs_days`. The raw log is `<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/jobs/<job_id>.log`, next to the record.

*Workers*: A job of kind `llm` is a worker (link:../model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc[Model Work Specification, Supervision & Recycling]): the headless harness CLI of the enrolled harness its role names (xref:11-enrolment-store.adoc#harness-enrolment-store[Harness Enrolment Store]), launched by the supervisor with the launch contract recorded in `worker` (its composed argument vector is digested in `argv_digest` like every job's), in the working directory fixed at its launch (a plan worktree or the project checkout), and the screener in the empty subdirectory `cwd/` of its job-private directory `<PM_MCP_BASE>/run/jobs/<job_id>/` (link:../filesystem-layout.adoc#job-private-directories[Filesystem Layout Specification, Job-Private Directories]). Its raw output is the job log; the server parses the per-turn usage and the run totals from it. The worker block holds the protocol state of the worker (its state, the task it holds, the skills its generation has received, its recycling counters); the usage of each attempt of a task is a metrics row of the task's scope (xref:05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store (`metrics.json`)]). A worker's generation is its `job_id`. The job token is valid only while `status` is `running` and is revoked in the fate transaction; a call over a revoked token is refused `job_ended`, and a call of a fenced generation `generation_fenced`.

[#lesson-record]
== Lesson Record (project lesson store)
_Implementation watch: link:../../implementation-watch/cli-and-security.adoc#PM-WATCH-CLI-4[PM-WATCH-CLI-4], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-14[PM-WATCH-STORE-14], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-15[PM-WATCH-STORE-15], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-16[PM-WATCH-STORE-16], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-17[PM-WATCH-STORE-17], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-18[PM-WATCH-STORE-18], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-19[PM-WATCH-STORE-19], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-20[PM-WATCH-STORE-20], link:../../implementation-watch/store-schemas.adoc#PM-WATCH-STORE-34[PM-WATCH-STORE-34]_

Lessons live in the project's lesson store in the per-project machine store and outlive the plans that create them. Each lesson is one Markdown file: YAML front matter carrying the fields below, followed by its Markdown body. The narrative stays Markdown text (`PM-IMPL-2`); only the front matter is structured. The lesson `submit` transition (link:../phase-workflows.adoc[Phase Workflows Specification], Lesson Creation & Body) writes both atomically. The store is a Ported store (`PM-TEST-1`; its legacy mapping lives in test code only, `PM-TEST-2`).

* *Placement*: The store is `<PM_MCP_BASE>/projects/<project_id>/lessons/` (link:../filesystem-layout.adoc#machine-store[Filesystem Layout Specification, Per-Project Machine Store]): machine-local, outside the repository, resolved identically from the main checkout and every worktree, one file `<lesson_id>.md` per lesson and the lesson-store digest sidecar `.digests.json`. A lesson file is never moved and never deleted by a lifecycle transition: applying a lesson claims it, and retiring it records the retirement in its front matter. Legacy lessons never enter it: `PM-MCP` never reads the project's legacy `.plan/local/lessons-learned/` (`PM-MIG-2`), and every project starts with an empty lesson store. A lesson therefore stays on the machine that recorded it; a second machine or clone does not see it. This trade-off is accepted: lessons reach other machines only through their promotion destinations (`promotions[]`).
* *Lock*: Lesson id allocation and every write, claim, release, or retirement in the lesson store run under the lesson store lock `locks/lessons.lock` of the per-project machine store (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#lock-free-snapshot-reads[Lock-Free Snapshot Reads, Transaction Locks & Leases (`PM-IMPL-7`)]), each as one journaled transaction (xref:01-format-and-session-log.adoc#transaction-journal[Transaction Journal (`mcp/transaction-journal.json`)]), so two concurrent sessions never allocate the same `lesson_id` and never claim the same lesson.
* *File Format*: A lesson file starts with a line `---`, followed by the front matter, a line `---`, and the Markdown body. The front matter is YAML restricted to the JSON data model (YAML 1.2 core schema; anchors, aliases, tags, and multiple documents are refused) and is validated against the `LessonFrontMatter` schema below, including `format_version`, on every read and write; a write with missing or invalid front matter is rejected, and a file without parseable front matter lists as `present_unreadable`. The body is free Markdown; the `title` lives only in the front matter.
* *Revisions*: A change of the body replaces it wholesale and increments `revision`; the new body carries its own `sources[]` (the evidence it was regenerated from), and `revisions[]` appends `{n, at, sources, body_sha256}` for the new revision. Old bodies are not kept; `revisions[]` keeps their digests and sources, so the derivation of every revision stays stated. A body is never edited in part (`PM-WATCH-STORE-17`).
* *Lesson-Store Digest*: Every server write, claim, release, or retirement in the lesson store records the size and SHA-256 of the lesson file in `.digests.json` (the sidecar format of xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#store-digest-verification[Store Digest Verification Against Out-of-Band Edits]) in the same transaction as the lesson write. On read, a lesson whose file differs from its entry raises `integrity_breach` (kind `store_tampered`) for that lesson only; a lesson file that disappeared while its entry remains is reported as `deleted_out_of_band`, and a file without an entry is reported as `untracked`. None of these states is ever read as `absent`, and an out-of-band edit is accepted only with `pm-operator accept-edit lesson <lesson_id>`, which shows the path and the recorded and current size and SHA-256 before confirmation, re-baselines that lesson's entry, and records the action in `audit.jsonl`. The breach of a lesson ends only by that `accept-edit` (no abort applies to the lesson store); until then the lesson is not listed as a candidate, `source-lesson` fails for it with `lesson_unavailable` (state `store_tampered`), and no claim, release, or retirement touches it.

* *One Resolver, Four States*: Listing and lookup use one resolver over the lesson store. Every listed lesson resolves by the exact id it is listed under to one of `found`, `absent`, `present_unreadable` (front matter missing or invalid; listed with its path, never omitted), or `empty_shell` (valid front matter, empty body). `present_unreadable` is never reported as `absent`, and `empty_shell` is never a content verdict. A lesson write without valid front matter is rejected.
* *Lifecycle*: `status` is `active`, `upstream_pending`, or `retired`; the lifecycle is binary (live or retired) and carries no moving confidence value, mirroring `plan-marshall`. Whether a lesson is being applied is its `claim`, never the location of its file.
* *Claim*: The `start.create-scope` transaction of a plan bootstrap with source `lesson` claims the lesson (workspace lock, plan lock, then lesson store lock): it sets `claim{plan_id, claimed_at}`; a lesson that is already claimed, retired, not resolved `found`, or in an integrity breach is refused (`lesson_unavailable`, naming the state and, for a claim, the claiming plan), and the bootstrap returns to `start.ready` with the reason. The claim ends with the plan: when the plan ends with outcome `merged`, the lesson is retired with verdict `applied` (`by`: the `plan_id`); when the plan ends `returned`, `handed_back`, `no_change`, or `aborted`, or is deleted (`delete-plan`), the claim is released (`claim: null`) and the lesson stays `active`. An obsolete lesson never reaches a plan: the premise state of `source-lesson` retires it with verdict `obsolete` (`by`: the `entry_id`) before any plan exists. A claim whose plan is terminal, archived, or no longer resolvable while the claim is still set is a *stale claim*: listed as such (the `stalled_lessons` harvester population), never released without one of the above events or an operator action.
* *arch-constraint Recurrence*: An add whose `rule` matches an active `arch-constraint` lesson reinforces it (increments `recurrence_count`, refreshes `last_seen`) instead of allocating a new lesson. The quiet-window retirement, performed by `pm-operator sweep` beside the retention purges (xref:13-leases-integrity-testing.adoc#evidence-based-removal-sweeps[Evidence-Based Removal Sweeps]), retires every active `arch-constraint` lesson whose `last_seen` is older than the window (`retention.arch_constraint_quiet_days` of the operator's `local-config.json`, link:../timeouts/03-retention-host-config.adoc#retention-days[Timeouts & Budgets Specification]) with verdict `obsolete`.
* *Retirement*: A retirement (supersede, merge, remove, quiet-window retirement, the premise `retire` of a lesson source, or the end of a claim) sets `retired{at, by, verdict, reason, …}` and `status: retired` in the front matter in one transaction; the file stays in place, so no separate tombstone exists. Every retirement other than the claim outcomes requires a `verdict` from the closed set, with no default. `completely_covered` also requires `covering_clause` and a concrete `covering_input` that the clause's worked example resolves; a whitespace-only value counts as missing. A retirement acts only on a lesson resolved `found`, or on a `present_unreadable` lesson through an explicit operator action, which replaces the unparseable front matter by a minimal valid one (`lesson_id` from the file name, `title` and `component` `unknown`, `retired.lesson_state: present_unreadable`) and keeps the original file bytes as the body; in every other state it changes nothing and reports the state. Retention purges only the body of a retired lesson after `retired_lesson_bodies_days`, whatever the retirement's verdict, and keeps its front matter.
* *Lesson Maintenance*: The sub-workflow unit `lessons.pmwf`, called from the workspace's `project-setup` (`setup.maintain-lessons`), writes the judgments of its `lessons.review` task (role `lessons`) only through the server. The task's entity runs the multi-submit entity unit `entity-multi.pmwf`: each of the following submits is validated and persisted on its own while the entity stays active, and the closing `submit:review` ends it (link:../model-work.adoc[Model Work Specification], Entity Units). `submit merge (ids, into)` appends the merged lessons to `merged_from[]` of `into`, replaces its body as a new revision, and retires each merged lesson (verdict `merged`, `into`); `submit supersede (id, by)` retires `id` with verdict `superseded` and `into: by`; `submit retire (id, verdict)` retires with the given verdict. Each is one transaction under the lesson store lock and acts only on lessons resolved `found` and unclaimed; a claimed lesson is refused `lesson_unavailable`.
* *No Bulk Mutation*: The lesson store is never wiped or bulk-mutated; no bulk-mutation verb exists. A removal answering `absent` is not proof that the lesson never existed and is never retried automatically; it is reported together with the store-resolution discriminator.
* *Version Provenance*: The runtime stamps `tool_version` from its own release version (`PM-DIST-1`) at creation; it is never a `submit` parameter. An operator override is stored in `tool_version_override` beside the stamped value.
* *Provenance*: Every new lesson records `provenance` (`run_artifact`, `tool_return`, or `operator_action`); the server sets it where the creating transition determines it (a recurring-waiver lesson of `lessons-capture` is `run_artifact`) and otherwise requires it as a `submit` parameter.
* *Audience Classification*: A lesson's `audience` is `upstream` iff its `component` names a component shipped by the `PM-MCP` distribution (a built-in skill, recipe, or finalize-step identifier from the compiled-in catalogue); every other component, including a project-prefixed one (`api-sheriff:maven-build`) and one without a bundle prefix (`integration-tests`), is `local`. The classification never inspects the repository layout. An operator override of the classification is persisted on the lesson in `audience_override` in addition to its audit record, so a lesson admitted by an override is never indistinguishable from a classified one. *Deliberate change vs. `plan-marshall`*: `manage-lessons add` decides ownership by the existence of `marketplace/bundles/{prefix}`, which misfiles a consumer's own prefixed component as foreign.
* *Upstream Release (`PM-ARCH-2`)*: An `upstream` lesson is created `upstream_pending` and stays in the local store; nothing transmits it automatically. It leaves the workstation only through the operator action `pm-operator lesson release --lesson <lesson_id>`, which renders the issue body for review and either hands it to the operator to file or, on the operator's explicit confirmation, runs a filing job through the enrolled CI provider. A `local` lesson is never offered this action; naming one is refused with `audience_mismatch`, which states the lesson's audience and leaves it unchanged. A successful filing records the issue in `promotions[]` (`kind: issue`) and returns the lesson to `active`.
** *Rendering*: The issue body is rendered from the stored record only: body, `component`, producing `plan_id`, and the recorded `tool_version`, never values sampled at filing time.
** *Duplicates*: Duplicate detection keys on (`component`, normalized `rule`) for `arch-constraint` lessons and on (`component`, normalized `title`) for every other category, never on `component` alone (two lessons on the same component are usually different defects); a `no_duplicate` verdict carries the compared population (count and ids).
** *Loud, Lossless Failure*: A failed filing leaves the lesson `upstream_pending` with `last_filing_failure` (`offline`, `forbidden`, `rate_limited`, or `refused`) and the unchanged content; it never falls back to `local`.
** *Corpus Routing Campaign*: A routing run over an existing lesson corpus (the `lesson-cleanup` recipe) records one disposition per lesson (`routed`, `reclassified`, or `retired` through the in-file retirement with its mandatory `reason` and `verdict`, *Retirement*); re-running on unchanged input is a no-op keyed on the duplicate key (*Duplicates*); when the population differs between derivation and action, the run reports the delta (added and removed ids) and never adopts it silently.
* *Promotion Provenance*: `promotions[]` records every destination that absorbed the lesson. A routing sweep or the `lesson-cleanup` harvester reports a lesson with a matching destination as `already_promoted` and never routes it again as new.
