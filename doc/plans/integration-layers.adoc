== What

* `pm-mcp-server`: no TCP or UDP listener, the refusal without the runtime token, restart recovery.
* `pm-e2e`, in the release layout: the relay rules at the process level, the relay restart in its generation, `hello` from a second connection, the operator checks and the runtime-free verbs against the real daemon, and one relay-to-daemon round trip.
