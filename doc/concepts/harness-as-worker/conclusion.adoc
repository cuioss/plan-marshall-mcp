The conclusion of roadmap Milestone 0, Part A (control direction and the pull mechanism, 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-03) and of Stages 1 and 2 of its evaluation (2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04). Antigravity's cells of the evaluation were not measured, because its quota ran out; on the operator's decision of 2026-10-04 they are extrapolated from the data at hand and not waited for. The session-first offer (xref:evaluation.adoc#e4[E4]) is the one item still to run.

== The answer in two questions

*Does the harness as worker work at all?* Yes. A headless harness process that the server starts, supervises, and replaces holds its pull loop for hours, loses and duplicates no task when it is killed, stalled, or ends its turn, takes the skills the server delivers as binding, consults other roles through the server, and runs where its role's configuration puts it. Supervision is not optional: neither the transport nor the model reports that a worker is lost or about to give up, and the server has to detect it from the process, the acknowledgement, and silence (E1, E2), and recycle workers before they drift or tire (E3). The interactive session cannot be a driver (V10). Model judgment holds for reviews and for triage of review comments and static-analysis findings; it does not yet hold for triage of build failures and for re-planning, which stay with the operator (E7).

*Does our instrumentation work?* Yes. Every figure comes from the stub's event log, the relay's host-side log, and the arrival-stamped output of the harness, never from what a model reports about itself. The fault injector produced every planned fault, and the job runtime detected each one within its deadline on all three harnesses (E2). The instrumentation found its own defects, which were fixed and recorded: a respawn storm when an account hit its usage limit (now a start-failure guard), tasks open at the end of a run counted as lost (now read from the stub's final state), a run-total record read as a context size (Antigravity), worker ids that clashed across roles. Where it could not measure, it says so: Antigravity's cells are marked extrapolated, a killed worker's usage unmeasured, a re-scored figure as resting on changed references. The verification corpus (`test/model/verification/`) keeps the verified questions for the next measurement.

The figures behind every statement below are in xref:measurements.adoc[Measurements]; the identifiers name the verification.

== Established

* A headless worker holds the pull loop (V2).
* Recovery is exact: a killed worker's task comes back exactly once, and a replaced worker's late submit is refused (V9).
* The lifecycle is controllable: workers end cleanly on `SIGTERM`, stored sessions resume (V4).
* Decisions are as good warm as fresh for closed decisions (V3).
* A fresh job is cheap enough as the default form of a small decision (V5).
* Usage is attributable per task, except on Antigravity (V7).
* Injected text does not travel from one task to the next (V8).
* Supervision works: a lost worker is detected (by exit at once, by silence within its deadline), its lease released at the fence, its task submitted exactly once by the successor, and every late submit of the replaced generation refused, on all three harnesses (E2).
* Recycling on idle and on the token budget keeps a worker from giving up, with no task waiting longer than one fresh start; under held waits even an unrecycled worker held four hours (E3).
* A worker can consult another role, on the same or another harness, through the server, and gets the answer through its wait (E5).
* Skills delivered by the server are binding: the skill rule was followed in every delivery form and re-delivered after every reset (E13).
* Reviews are model work that holds: security, simplify, and self-review found every seeded defect and nothing in the clean controls, warm and fresh; triage of review comments and static-analysis findings reaches the operator's level (E7).
* Roles are configuration: each role ran where its configuration put it, and a change of configuration moved roles between harnesses without a code change (E8).
* The screener as specified, a fresh zero-tool job after a deterministic level 1, catches injected instructions at a low false-positive rate; a warm screener does not stay unaffected (E9).

== Ruled out or limited

* The interactive session is not a driver: it pauses or stops on its own and queues the operator's input while looping (V10). Whether it can take tasks beside a headless fallback is E4, still to run.
* A worker left idle in short waits gives up (V2, the OpenCode model); held waits and idle recycling remove that (E3), so the model is eligible as a supervised worker.
* A warm context has a ceiling beyond which the loop breaks, lower for small models (V3); the token budget recycles below it (E3).
* The server learns nothing from the transport about a lost worker (V9, xref:findings.adoc[Findings]); it learns it from the process, the acknowledgement, and silence (E1, E2).
* The acknowledgement deadline cannot be short on every host: OpenCode's provider stalled a live worker for 120 s, so there the acknowledgement is informational and silence decides (E1).
* Small models can break their own tool calls; the server must check every submit against the link's schema (E5).
* Triage of build failures and re-planning did not reach the operator's level on the small model or on the larger one; they stay with the operator until more cases show otherwise (E7). A warm worker drifted on Sonar triage where a fresh job did not.
* A case whose facts lack the evidence its reference rests on is decided wrongly by every model: the facts a server composes for a decision must include that evidence (E7).
* Hosts differ in their limits, and progress does not help on every host (V1).
* Antigravity is the weakest worker host (V1, V5, V7, V9); its supervision behaves as on the other hosts (E2), its other cells are extrapolated, and several concurrent jobs behind its one global entry are shown only in a smoke run (E6).

== Consequences

The measurements lead to one architecture: the server supervises its workers, a worker follows one task protocol, and the work is divided into roles that are configuration, warm where frequent small decisions pay for it and fresh otherwise (xref:architecture.adoc[Architecture], xref:task-protocol.adoc[Task Protocol], xref:roles.adoc[Roles]); the client logic reaches every worker as skills the server chooses and delivers (xref:skills.adoc[Skills]). Requirements that hold whatever the direction, on isolation, the relay, and the client profiles, follow from xref:findings.adoc[Findings].

== The decision gate

The operator decided on 2026-10-04 (xref:analysis.adoc#gate-decisions[Analysis, Decisions]), in line with the evidence:

. *Direction*: the server drives the interior of plans and epics through supervised headless workers (option 2); the interactive session is entry and viewer.
. *Form of small decisions*: fresh jobs by default; supervised warm workers for roles with frequent small decisions.
. *Deterministic server logic*: the remediation `decide` state with a single bound action; every model decision costs the seconds of a model turn (V5, V7).
. *Watching*: the web application and `pm-operator status` are the dependable views; the session may watch.

Applying the decisions to the requirements, specifications, and Milestones 2 to 14 is the follow-up plan of xref:evaluation.adoc[Evaluation, Next steps outside this plan].
