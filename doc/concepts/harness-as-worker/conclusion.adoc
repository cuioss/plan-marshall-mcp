The conclusion of roadmap Milestone 0, Part A (control direction and the pull mechanism, 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-03), updated with Stage 1 of the evaluation (2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04). The decision gate of xref:analysis.adoc#gate[Analysis] is the operator's; this document states what the measurements establish, what they rule out, and the design that follows from them.

== The answer in one paragraph

The server can be the driver. A headless harness process that the server starts holds a pull loop for hours, survives being killed without losing or duplicating its task, keeps injected text from travelling, and reports its usage per task; a fresh job for a small decision is cheap. What breaks is the model's willingness to keep pulling: when nothing arrives for a long time, when its context grows large, or when it runs inside an interactive session. Neither the transport nor the model reports that it happened. The server therefore has to supervise its workers, and the interactive session cannot be a driver. Stage 1 of the evaluation measured that supervision: the server detects every lost worker, replaces it, and gives its task to the successor exactly once; recycling keeps workers from giving up; consultation and skill delivery work through the server. What is still open is the session-first offer, Antigravity as a concurrent job host, and the quality of open-ended decisions (xref:evaluation.adoc[Evaluation]).

The figures behind every statement below are in xref:measurements.adoc[Measurements]; the identifiers name the verification.

== Established

* A headless worker holds the pull loop (V2).
* Recovery is exact: a killed worker's task comes back exactly once, and a replaced worker's late submit is refused (V9).
* The lifecycle is controllable: workers end cleanly on `SIGTERM`, stored sessions resume (V4).
* Decisions are as good warm as fresh (V3).
* A fresh job is cheap enough as the default form of a small decision (V5).
* Usage is attributable per task, except on Antigravity (V7).
* Injected text does not travel from one task to the next (V8).
* Supervision works: a lost worker is detected (by exit at once, by silence within its deadline), its lease released at the fence, its task submitted exactly once by the successor, and every late submit of the replaced generation refused, on all three harnesses (E2).
* Recycling on idle and on the token budget keeps a worker from giving up, with no task waiting longer than one fresh start (E3).
* A worker can consult another role, on the same or another harness, through the server, and gets the answer through its wait (E5).
* Skills delivered by the server are binding: the skill rule was followed in every delivery form and re-delivered after every reset (E13).
* Reviews are model work that holds: security, simplify, and self-review found every seeded defect and nothing in the clean controls, warm and fresh (E7).
* Roles are configuration: each role ran where its configuration put it, and a change of configuration moved roles between harnesses without a code change (E8).
* The screener as specified, a fresh zero-tool job after a deterministic level 1, catches injected instructions at a low false-positive rate; a warm screener does not stay unaffected (E9).

== Ruled out or limited

* The interactive session is not a driver: it pauses or stops on its own and queues the operator's input while looping (V10).
* A worker left idle gives up; the OpenCode model is not eligible as a puller (V2).
* A warm context has a ceiling beyond which the loop breaks, lower for small models (V3).
* The server learns nothing from the transport about a lost worker (V9, xref:findings.adoc[Findings]); it learns it from the process, the acknowledgement, and silence (E1, E2).
* The acknowledgement deadline cannot be short on every host: OpenCode's provider stalled a live worker for 120 s, so there the acknowledgement is informational and silence decides (E1).
* Small models can break their own tool calls; the server must check every submit against the link's schema (E5).
* Open-ended triage of build failures and PR comments, and re-planning, did not reach the operator's level on the small model or on the larger one; they stay with the operator until more and reviewed cases show otherwise (E7). A warm worker drifted on Sonar triage where a fresh job did not.
* Hosts differ in their limits, and progress does not help on every host (V1).
* Antigravity is the weakest worker host (V1, V5, V7, V9).

== Consequences

The measurements lead to one architecture: the server supervises its workers, a worker follows one task protocol, and the work is divided into typed roles, warm where frequent small decisions pay for it and fresh otherwise (xref:architecture.adoc[Architecture], xref:task-protocol.adoc[Task Protocol], xref:roles.adoc[Roles]); the client logic reaches every worker as skills the server chooses and delivers (xref:skills.adoc[Skills]). Requirements that hold whatever the direction, on isolation, the relay, and the client profiles, follow from xref:findings.adoc[Findings].

== For the decision gate

Read against the four decisions of the gate:

. *Direction*: the evidence supports the server driving the interior of plans and epics through supervised headless workers (option 2 of xref:analysis.adoc#options[Analysis]), with the interactive session as entry and viewer. It speaks against any design that depends on the interactive session to keep pulling.
. *Form of small decisions*: fresh jobs by default; supervised warm workers for roles with frequent small decisions; resuming a stored session is possible but brings back the context that recycling removes.
. *Deterministic server logic*: every model decision costs the seconds of a model turn whatever its form (V5, V7), which is the price of leaving a decision with a single bound action to a model.
. *Watching a running plan*: the interactive session can watch, and a long call does not block it, but it cannot be relied on to react promptly; the web application and `pm-operator status` are the dependable views.

The supervision protocol, recycling, consultation through the server, and skill delivery are measured (Stage 1); the quality of the roles, the routing of roles by configuration, and the screener form are measured (Stage 2). The session-first offer and Antigravity as a concurrent job host and review host are open (xref:evaluation.adoc[Evaluation]).
